About My Journey

Graduated in May 2025 with a BSc in GIS and Remote Sensing. Shortly after acquiring my KCAA license, I joined TOA Corporation as the sole drone pilot for the Mombasa Special Economic Zone Port sub-project contracted by KPA. Starting with 5 hours of flight time, I have expanded my scope to 70+ flight hours, managing complex aerial photogrammetry and underwater ROV operations in highly controlled airspace near Mtongwe Naval Base and Moi International Airport.

Core Technical Expertise

  • Aerial Photogrammetry & Topography: Generating high-resolution orthomosaics, 3D meshes, and site volumetric calculations.
  • Underwater ROV Operations: Operating the Fifish V6 Expert ROV for underwater structural inspection and marine assessment.
  • Environmental Monitoring: Monitoring water turbidity during dredging and disposal operations.
  • Airspace Clearance & Coordination: Liaison with KCAA, KPA, and accompanying naval officers for operating within restricted zones.
  • Video Editing & VIP Presentations: Producing high-level progress video presentations for official client updates and VIP delegation visits.
